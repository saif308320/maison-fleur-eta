Absolute Rigor

Authentication & Certification

Every timepiece admitted to Chronos Maison undergoes a rigorous 6-stage forensic examination. Using high-magnification spectrometry, movement amplitude analysis, and archival cross-referencing, we guarantee 100% authenticity.

1

Archival Provenance Verification

Cross-referencing serial registries and original factory ledgers.

2

Non-Destructive Metallurgical Assay

Ensuring case metal purity and verifying unpolished chamfers.

3

Chronometric Bench Testing

State-of-the-art Witschi timing machine calibration across 6 positions.

Horological Restoration Services

Our atelier is equipped with Swiss-certified machinery and staffed by master horologists trained in the restoration of antique pocket watches and mid-century complications.

Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about acquisition, shipping, and authentication.

How are timepieces authenticated?

Every watch undergoes a 6-stage verification process including archival serial check, metallurgical assay, and Witschi chronometer testing by our master watchmakers.

Are shipments fully insured?

Yes. All shipments are dispatched via specialized secure armored couriers (Brink's or Malca-Amit) with 100% full-value transit insurance.

Can I sell or trade my current timepiece?

Certainly. We offer both direct immediate buyout and private global consignment options with competitive commission rates.

Do watches come with a warranty?

All pieces purchased from Chronos Maison include a 2-year mechanical warranty certified by our in-house atelier.
